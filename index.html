Delay Lab

DD-3 digital delay (up to 2000 ms) and ping pong delay for your own audio. Play through a mic live, load a file, or record a take and process it afterwards.

Source

Use wired headphones. A mic next to speakers will howl as soon as the feedback goes up. Bluetooth headphones add 100–200 ms of their own delay, so live playing feels late.

Record take saves your dry signal. It then opens in the Audio file tab, so you can try every effect on it and download the result.
